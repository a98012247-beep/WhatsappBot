WhatsApp Commands

Quick reference for commands available in WhatsApp chats and groups

!menu / !help

Main Menu

Lists everything the bot can help with directly in WhatsApp.

!surah <name/number>

Quran Surah Info

Complete information of any Surah (1-114) with Arabic title, Urdu meaning, Makki/Madani, and total ayahs. E.g.: !surah Yasin

!ayah <surah:ayah>

Quran Ayah & Translation

Arabic text with authentic Urdu (Jalandhry) and English translation. E.g.: !ayah 2:255 (Ayat-ul-Kursi) or !ayah 36:1

!tafseer <surah:ayah>

Quran Tafseer (Tafheem-ul-Quran)

Detailed Urdu Tafseer by Maulana Maududi for any Ayah. E.g.: !tafseer 1:1 or !tafseer 112:1

!tilawat <surah> [qari]

Quran Audio MP3 Recitation

Sends full audio MP3 file directly to WhatsApp. Reciters: Mishary Alafasy, Sudais, Abdul Basit, Ghamdi. E.g.: !tilawat Rahman

!hadith <book> <number>

Hadith Finder (All 6 Sahih Books)

Arabic, Urdu & English text from Bukhari (1-7563), Muslim (1-7563), Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Nasai, Ibn Majah. E.g.: !hadith bukhari 1

!namaz <city>

Namaz Timings & Hijri Date

Accurate Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha prayer times for any city worldwide. E.g.: !namaz Lahore

!date / !hijri

Islamic Date & Holy Events

Live accurate Hijri date, Islamic month fazeelat, and major Islamic festivals (Ramadan, Eid, Ashura, Shab-e-Qadr). E.g.: !hijri or !events

!allahnames & !dua

99 Names & Masnoon Duas

Browse 99 Asma-ul-Husna with Urdu meanings or fetch daily Masnoon Duas with Arabic, pronunciation & translation.

!song <name>

Songs & Music MP3

Search & play any song, music or naat in crystal clear MP3 format. E.g.: !song tum hi ho or natural: song tajdar e haram

!photo <name>

Photos & Wallpapers

Search HD pictures and wallpapers. E.g.: !photo lahore or natural: cute cat pic, wallpaper nature

!s / !sticker

Photo to Sticker Converter

Reply to any photo with !s or send an image with caption !s to generate an instant transparent WhatsApp sticker.

!audio / !v2a

Video to Audio (MP3)

Reply to any video with !audio or provide a video link to extract and send clean audio. E.g.: !audio or !audio https://...

!meme / !soundboard

Viral Meme Soundboard
